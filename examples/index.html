What is optimized?

Total route cost with distance coefficient 1, time coefficient 0, fixed cost 0, and weight capacity enforced.

What is checked?

Visit coverage, vehicle capacity, route distances, result semantics, and the independent total cost.

What is not claimed?

A production benchmark, road-network distance, all-industry compatibility, or optimality certification.

worker.scheduling · 0.1.0

Worker assignment: respect time and availability.

Three fixed-time tasks, two workers. Minimize assignment cost while preserving eligibility, availability and workload.

optimal · 7 cost_unit

TaskFixed timeAssigned worker
pack0–60 minalice
inspect30–90 minbob
ship90–150 minalice

Availability, eligibility, overlap and workload are checked independently. This assigns existing fixed-time tasks; it does not generate shifts. The bounded optimum is checked by a separate exhaustive oracle.

inventory.replenishment · 0.1.0

Inventory: order before demand arrives.

One product over four days. Balance order, setup and holding costs while respecting lead time and storage.

optimal · 16 cost_unit

ProductOrders by dayEnding stock by day
widget1, 5, 0, 00, 0, 2, 0

Stock balance, capacity, terminal stock and cost are checked independently. Demand is supplied input; the engine does not forecast demand. The bounded optimum is checked by a separate exhaustive oracle.

From CSV to a checked worker assignment

Use a separate CSV dataset to compare baseline, changed assignment cost and changed availability. Expected synthetic outcomes are costs 12 and 14, followed by a proven infeasible scenario with no cost. The command writes assignment tables and exact transcripts.

.agent/bin/python -I -B worker_analysis.py --workers worker-workers.csv --tasks worker-tasks.csv --costs worker-costs.csv --scenarios worker-scenarios.json --output worker-analysis-first --run-id worker-first
