What is optimized?

Total route cost with distance coefficient 1, time coefficient 0, fixed cost 0, and weight capacity enforced.

What is checked?

Visit coverage, vehicle capacity, route distances, result semantics, and the independent total cost.

What is not claimed?

A production benchmark, road-network distance, all-industry compatibility, or optimality certification.
